Migration is free. We rebuild your programs and check every client before you invite them.

In setup step 2, under **Want us to do it?**, choose **Email the migration team** and attach (or tell us how to get) your exports:

- your client list, and
- your clients' programs.

You'll hear back by email. Nothing is sent to your clients until you invite them.
