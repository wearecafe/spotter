Bringing your clients over is step 2 of setup, **Bring your clients**. Pick where you coach today and Spotter shows exactly what can come across.

## What comes across

| From | Clients | Programs | Past workout logs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **TrueCoach** | Names and emails from the client CSV export | Read from workouts you email to yourself or print to PDF | Only what's in the files you export |
| **Trainerize** | Names and contact details from the client CSV | Read from the PDFs you save, matched to the exercise library | Not included: Trainerize's export leaves them out |
| **Spreadsheets** | Any sheet with names and emails, saved as CSV | Written one exercise per line, like "Back Squat 4x5 75kg" | Not imported yet; keep the sheet for reference |
| **Starting fresh** | Add clients one at a time from Clients | Build programs with progression rules from scratch | — |

## How it works

1. Upload your **client list** (CSV), your **program files**, or both. See [Importing your client list](/help/switching/importing-clients) and [Importing programs](/help/switching/importing-programs).
2. Review the import: check the exercise names Spotter matched and confirm the rest.
3. Finish the import. Each client's program is rebuilt as a Spotter program, ready for progression rules.
4. Invite your clients in the last step of setup. Their invite explains that their coaching is moving to Spotter and their current program is already there.

> **Tip** Don't want to do it yourself? Migration is free and done for you. See [Done-for-you migration](/help/switching/done-for-you-migration).
