Clients log one entry per day (weight, calories, protein, carbs and fat) in Spotter, or import days from the app they already use.

| App | What to upload |
| --- | --- |
| **MacroFactor** | The CSV from MacroFactor's data export. |
| **MyFitnessPal** | The .zip from MyFitnessPal's data export, or the Nutrition Summary CSV inside it. MyFitnessPal may require Premium to export. |
| **Lose It!** | The food log CSV from Lose It!'s data export. |
| **Anything else** | Any CSV with a Date column plus Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat or Weight columns. |

Importing again is safe: days already in Spotter are updated, not duplicated.

## Why there's no live sync

None of these apps offers an open connection for other apps to read from, so clients export a file and upload it. They can do this as often as they like, for example once a week before their check-in.
