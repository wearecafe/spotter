Open a client's page and choose **Nutrition**.

## Set targets

Under **Daily targets**, enter calories and protein, carbs and fat in grams. Spotter shows what the macros add up to in calories, so you can check they match. Choose **Save targets**; the client sees them in their app.

## Averages

- **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days** averages for calories and macros, against the targets.
- How many of those days they logged.
- Their **weight** change over the period.

## Daily log

Each day's weight, calories and macros, and where it came from (logged in Spotter or imported from an app).

Weekly check-in summaries in your inbox include the week's nutrition.
