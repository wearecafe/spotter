## Log in

Choose **Log in** on the Spotter home page and enter your email and password. Tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on that device.

Coaches and clients use the same login page. Spotter takes you to the right place: coaches to the Overview page, clients to their workouts.

## Forgot your password

1. On the login page, choose **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email. Spotter emails you a reset link.
3. Open the link and choose a new password.

If the email doesn't arrive in a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## Change your password

Open **Settings**, then **Security**. Under **Update password**, enter your current password and the new one.

## Clients who haven't set a password

Clients set their password when they accept their invite. If a client lost their invite, send them the link from their client page again. See [Inviting clients to the app](/help/clients/inviting-clients).
