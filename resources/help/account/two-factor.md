Two-factor authentication asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (like Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy) each time you log in.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings**, then **Security** (you’ll be asked for your password first).
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, choose **Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the setup key.
4. Enter the code the app shows to confirm.
5. Choose **View recovery codes** and save them somewhere safe, like a password manager. Each one lets you sign in once if you lose your phone. **Regenerate codes** gives you a fresh set.

## Turn it off

In the same place, choose **Disable 2FA**.
