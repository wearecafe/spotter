## Profile

Open **Settings**, then **Profile**, to change your name and email.

## Appearance

Under **Appearance**, choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device.

## Language

- **Clients** use Spotter in English or French. They choose when they accept their invite, and their emails come in that language. Imported clients can have a language set in the client list. See [Importing your client list](/help/switching/importing-clients).
- **The coach side** of Spotter is in English. Drafted replies to French-speaking clients are written in French.
- In setup, **Languages you coach in** records whether you coach in English, French or both.

To change a client's language after they've joined, contact Spotter support.
