---
title: Deleting your account
summary: Permanently delete your Spotter account, and what to do first if you coach clients.
order: 4
related: teams/removing-a-coach, clients/reassigning-and-removing
---

Open **Settings**, then **Profile**, and choose **Delete account**. Enter your password to confirm. Your account and its data are deleted permanently.

> **Heads up** For coaches, deleting your account also deletes the programs, workouts and messages you created for your clients. If you're leaving a gym, ask an owner or admin to remove you from the team instead: your clients move to another coach with their history. See [Removing a coach](/help/teams/removing-a-coach).

If you're a client and want to stop training with your coach, talk to them: they can remove you, which deletes your account and history.
