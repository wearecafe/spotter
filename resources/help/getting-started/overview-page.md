The Overview page is where you land after signing in.

## The numbers

- **Inbox**: how many items are waiting for you, or "All caught up".
- **Active clients**: clients you can see. Owners and admins see the whole team's.
- **Scheduled today**: workouts on the calendar for today.
- **Completed this week**: workouts finished since Monday.
- **Missed (last 7 days)**: past workouts in the last week that weren't completed.

## Needs attention

Clients show up here when they missed a workout in the last 7 days or have nothing scheduled from today on. Open a client to schedule something or check in with them. When everyone is on track, the list says so.

## Recent activity

The latest completed workouts across your clients, newest first.
