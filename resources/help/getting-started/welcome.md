Spotter is coaching software for personal trainers and gyms. You write the programming, your clients train from it (on their phone or with you in the gym), and Spotter brings you only what needs a coach.

## The main parts

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Programs | Build a training week once. Spotter puts every session on the client's calendar and plans the loads from your progression rules. |
| Client app | Clients see today's workout, log every set with reps, load and RPE, send form-check videos and a weekly check-in, track nutrition and message you. |
| Train | Run a session in the gym for one client or a small group, and log it for them. |
| Coach Inbox | Check-ins, form checks, workout feedback, new personal bests, missed sessions and messages, each with a reply already drafted. |
| Nutrition | Daily targets, and what clients log or import from MacroFactor, MyFitnessPal or Lose It!. |
| Teams | Owners, admins and coaches in one gym, sharing an exercise library and templates. |

## How a week usually goes

1. You build a [program](/help/programming/creating-a-program) for a client, with a [progression rule](/help/programming/progression-rules) on each exercise.
2. The client [logs each workout](/help/for-clients/logging-a-workout) in the app, or you [run the session in the gym](/help/in-the-gym/running-a-session) and log it for them.
3. When they finish, Spotter re-plans the coming weeks from what they actually lifted.
4. Anything worth a reply lands in your [Coach Inbox](/help/inbox-messages/coach-inbox). You edit the draft, approve it, and move on.

## In English and French

Clients use the app in English or French. They choose when they accept their invite, and invite emails go out in their language. See [Inviting clients to the app](/help/clients/inviting-clients).
