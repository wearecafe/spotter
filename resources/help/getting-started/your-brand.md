Clients see your business, not Spotter. Set your brand in the **Your brand** step of setup.

## What you can set

- **Brand colour**: pick a colour or enter your own.
- **Logo**: optional. An image up to 2 MB.
- **Web address**: your Spotter address is reserved for you now. Custom web addresses switch on before public launch.

The setup step shows a preview of the client app with your colour as you change it.

## In a gym

Clients see the team owner's brand: every coach's clients see the gym's name and colour, not the individual coach's.
