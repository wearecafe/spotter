Everything for coaches is in the sidebar. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top.

## Coaching

- **Overview**: today at a glance, who needs attention, and recent workouts. See [The Overview page](/help/getting-started/overview-page).
- **Train**: run a session in the gym and log it for your clients. See [Running a session for a client](/help/in-the-gym/running-a-session).
- **Inbox**: everything that needs a coach, with replies drafted. The number shows how many items are waiting.
- **Messages**: every conversation with your clients. The number shows unread messages.
- **Clients**: everyone you coach. Open a client to see their programs, schedule, history, nutrition and notes.
- **Team**: only for owners and admins. Coaches, roles and invitations. See [Owners, admins and coaches](/help/teams/roles).

## Library

- **Workout templates**: workouts you build once and schedule for anyone. See [Workout templates](/help/programming/templates).
- **Exercises**: the Spotter library plus your team's own exercises. See [The exercise library](/help/programming/exercise-library).

## Settings

Open your name at the bottom of the sidebar for **Settings** (profile, password, two-factor authentication and appearance) and **Log out**.
