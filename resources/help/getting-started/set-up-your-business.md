When you create a coach account, Spotter walks you through setup. You can leave at any step and come back from **Continue setup** on the Overview page.

## The steps

1. **Your business.** Your business name, billing currency (CAD or USD), the languages you coach in (English, French or both) and roughly how many active clients you coach. These set your defaults; you can change them later.
2. **Bring your clients.** Pick where you coach today (TrueCoach, Trainerize, spreadsheets or starting fresh). Spotter shows exactly what can come across, then you upload your client list and program files. See [Moving to Spotter](/help/switching/moving-to-spotter).
3. **Payments.** Taking payments from clients in Spotter is coming soon. Until then, keep billing clients the way you do today.
4. **Your brand.** Your colour, logo and web address. See [Your brand on the client app](/help/getting-started/your-brand).
5. **Invite clients.** Send invites to everyone, a few at a time, or skip for now. Each client gets the invite in their own language.

> **Tip** You don't have to import anything to get started. Pick "starting fresh", then add clients one at a time from the Clients page.

## Coming back to setup

Until setup is finished, the Overview page shows **Finish setting up Spotter**. Choose **Continue setup** to open it again: your answers are kept, and the list of setup steps lets you jump straight to any step.
