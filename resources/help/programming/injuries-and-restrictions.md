When a client has an injury or a limitation, record it on their page. Spotter then flags exercises that run into it wherever you build their training.

## Add a restriction

1. Open the client's page.
2. Under **Injuries and restrictions**, pick one from the list, for example **Knee: No deep knee bends** or **Shoulder: Nothing overhead**.
3. Pick the side (left, right or both) and add a note if it helps, like "cleared for partial range".
4. Choose **Add**.

Remove one with the **×** next to it once it no longer applies.

## Where exercises are flagged

- **Program page**: a warning under each exercise that runs into a restriction, and a reminder of the client's restrictions at the top.
- **Workout builder**: the same, for single workouts and individual sessions.
- **Exercise picker**: a warning under any exercise in the search results that runs into one, before you add it.

> **Heads up** Flags are matched on exercise names, so they catch the common lifts but can miss one or flag one that's fine for your client. Use them as a prompt, not a medical screen.

## Swap an exercise everywhere in a program

1. Open the program.
2. On the exercise's row, choose the **swap** button (two arrows).
3. Pick the replacement.

It's replaced on every day of the program it appears on, keeping the sets, reps, load and rule. Sessions already done or started keep the original, and so do sessions you edited by hand: Spotter tells you how many of those are still to come so you can check them.
