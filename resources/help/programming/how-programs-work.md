A program is a blueprint for one client. It has training **days** (Lower A, Upper A…), each on a fixed weekday, with its own exercises and a [progression rule](/help/programming/progression-rules) on each one.

## Spotter schedules every session

When you create a program, every session for every week goes onto the client's calendar. They see today's session in their app; you see them under **Upcoming** on their client page.

## Edit once, every upcoming week follows

Change the program on its page and every upcoming session follows: new exercises, different sets, a day moved to another weekday. Two kinds of sessions are left alone:

- **Started** sessions: the client (or you, in the gym) has logged something, or it's completed.
- **Edited** sessions: ones you changed by hand. See [Changing a single session](/help/programming/editing-one-session).

Logged history is never changed.

## Loads plan themselves

Each week's load or reps are planned from your progression rules. When the client finishes a session, Spotter re-plans the coming weeks from what they actually did: hit the targets and the next week steps up; miss and it holds.

## Programs or single workouts?

Use a program for anything that repeats week to week. For a one-off session, or a template repeated for a few weeks without progression, see [Scheduling a one-off or repeating workout](/help/programming/single-workouts).
