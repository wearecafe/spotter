---
title: Scheduling a one-off or repeating workout
summary: Put a single workout on a client's calendar, from a template or from scratch, once or weekly.
order: 6
related: programming/templates, programming/how-programs-work
---

For a workout that isn't part of a program:

1. Open the client's page.
2. Under **Schedule a workout**, pick a template, or **Blank workout** to build from scratch.
3. Pick the **date**.
4. Under **Repeat**, choose **Just once** or **Weekly** for 2 to 12 weeks.
5. Choose **Schedule**.

A blank workout opens straight away so you can add exercises.

Each scheduled copy is its own workout: change one and the others stay the same. Repeating workouts don't progress on their own. For week-to-week progression, use a [program](/help/programming/how-programs-work).
