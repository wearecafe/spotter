Open **Library**, **Exercises**.

## The Spotter library

More than 870 exercises, most with start and end photos, step-by-step instructions, the muscles worked, equipment and level. Clients see the photos and steps through **How to** in their workout.

Filter by **muscle group**, **equipment** and **level**, or search by name.

## Your own exercises

Choose **New exercise** and enter a name, muscle group, equipment, coaching cues (one step per line) and, optionally, a **demo video** link. Clients get a **Watch demo video** button.

To adjust a library exercise, choose **Copy to edit**. Your copy is a custom exercise you can change; the original stays as it is.

Use the **Library** filter to show everything, only **Your team's**, or only the **Spotter library**. Custom exercises are marked **Custom**.

## In a gym

Custom exercises are shared with every coach on your team.
