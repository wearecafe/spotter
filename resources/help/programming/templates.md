Templates live under **Library**, **Workout templates**.

## Create a template

1. Enter a name (like "Upper body A") and choose **Create**.
2. Add exercises with sets, reps, load and rest, and save.

You can also turn any client's workout into a template: open it and choose **Save as template**.

## Use a template

On a client's page, pick it under **Schedule a workout**. See [Scheduling a one-off or repeating workout](/help/programming/single-workouts).

## Manage templates

From a template's menu:

- **Edit** or **Duplicate** your own.
- **Copy to my templates** for a teammate's template.
- **Delete**. Workouts already scheduled from it are kept.

In a gym, every coach sees the team's templates, marked with who made them. Owners and admins can edit any of them.
