## Add one client

1. Open **Clients** and choose **Add client**.
2. Enter their **name** and **email**, and optionally a **goal** (like "First pull-up" or "Lose 10 lb"). The goal shows on their client page and at the top of their app.
3. If you're an owner or admin in a gym, pick their **Coach**. Leave it on **Me** to coach them yourself.
4. Tick **In person only** if they won't use the app and you'll log their sessions in the gym. See [In-person clients](/help/clients/in-person-clients).
5. Choose **Add client**. Spotter opens their client page.

Every client needs an email address, and each email can only belong to one Spotter account.

## Add many at once

Upload a client list (CSV) during setup instead. See [Importing your client list](/help/switching/importing-clients).

## What happens next

A new client who'll use the app shows an **Invited** badge until they accept. Their client page has an invite link to send them. See [Inviting clients to the app](/help/clients/inviting-clients).
