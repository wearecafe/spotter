## Move a client to another coach

Owners and admins can do this, in a gym with more than one coach.

1. Open the client's page.
2. Under **Coach**, pick the new coach.

Their upcoming workouts, programs and open inbox items move with them. Their history stays with them too.

## Stopped coaching someone?

Archive them instead of deleting them. Archiving keeps their history, takes them out of your lists and inbox, and stops them counting toward your bill from next month. See [Archiving a client](/help/clients/archiving-clients).

## Delete a client permanently

Only for when a client asks you to erase their data.

1. Open the client's page.
2. Under **Delete permanently**, choose **Delete client** and confirm.

> **Heads up** Deleting a client erases their account and all of their workouts, check-ins, habits and messages. It can't be undone.
