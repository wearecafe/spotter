## Move a client to another coach

Owners and admins can do this, in a gym with more than one coach.

1. Open the client's page.
2. Under **Coach**, pick the new coach.

Their upcoming workouts, programs and open inbox items move with them. Their history stays with them too.

## Remove a client

1. Open the client's page.
2. Choose **Remove client** and confirm.

> **Heads up** Removing a client deletes their account and all of their workout history. It can't be undone.
