Open **Clients** and choose a client to see their page.

## Along the top

- **Run session**: run today's session with them in the gym. See [Running a session for a client](/help/in-the-gym/running-a-session).
- **Progress**: how every lift has moved across all their programs. See [A client's progress](/help/clients/progress).
- **Nutrition**: their targets and daily log. See [Nutrition targets and averages](/help/nutrition/nutrition-targets).
- **Messages**: your conversation with them.
- Their **30-day compliance**. See [Compliance and who needs attention](/help/clients/compliance).

## Programs and workouts

- **Programs**: each program with the week they're on. Create a new one here. See [Creating a program](/help/programming/creating-a-program).
- **Schedule a workout**: a single workout from a template or blank, once or weekly. See [Scheduling a one-off or repeating workout](/help/programming/single-workouts).
- **Upcoming**: what's on their calendar. Open one to edit it, or remove it. Sessions the client moved show the day they moved them from.
- **History**: past workouts marked **Done** or **Missed**, with their feedback, who logged it if a trainer did, and session notes.

## Injuries, habits and profile

- **Injuries and restrictions**: what they should avoid. Exercises that run into these are flagged when you build their training. See [Injuries and restrictions](/help/programming/injuries-and-restrictions).
- **Habits**: daily or weekly targets like steps or water, with how this week is going. See [Habits](/help/clients/habits).
- **Goal**: shown at the top of their app.
- **Can move sessions**: whether they can reschedule a session to another day in the same week. On by default. See [Moving a session to another day](/help/for-clients/moving-a-session).
- **Private notes**: only coaches can see these. In a gym, other coaches who can see the client can read them too.

## Coach, archiving and deleting

- Owners and admins can change the client's **Coach** here.
- **Archive** when you stop coaching them: their history is kept and they come off your bill from next month. See [Archiving a client](/help/clients/archiving-clients).
- **Delete client** erases them and all of their history for good, for erasure requests only. See [Reassigning or deleting a client](/help/clients/reassigning-and-removing).
