Open **Clients** and choose a client to see their page.

## Along the top

- **Run session**: run today's session with them in the gym. See [Running a session for a client](/help/in-the-gym/running-a-session).
- **Nutrition**: their targets and daily log. See [Nutrition targets and averages](/help/nutrition/nutrition-targets).
- **Messages**: your conversation with them.
- Their **30-day compliance**. See [Compliance and who needs attention](/help/clients/compliance).

## Programs and workouts

- **Programs**: each program with the week they're on. Create a new one here. See [Creating a program](/help/programming/creating-a-program).
- **Schedule a workout**: a single workout from a template or blank, once or weekly. See [Scheduling a one-off or repeating workout](/help/programming/single-workouts).
- **Upcoming**: what's on their calendar. Open one to edit it, or remove it.
- **History**: past workouts marked **Done** or **Missed**, with their feedback, who logged it if a trainer did, and session notes.

## Profile and notes

- **Goal**: shown at the top of their app.
- **Private notes**: only coaches can see these. In a gym, other coaches who can see the client can read them too.

## Coach and removal

Owners and admins can change the client's **Coach** here. **Remove client** deletes their account and all of their history. See [Reassigning or removing a client](/help/clients/reassigning-and-removing).
