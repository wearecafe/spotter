Some clients only ever train with you in the gym. Mark them **In person only** and Spotter won't ask you to invite them. You run their sessions from **Train** and log every set for them.

## Mark a client as in person

- **When adding them:** tick **In person only** in the Add a client form.
- **Later:** on a client who hasn't joined yet, choose **In person only** in the "Waiting to join" box on their client page.

In-person clients show an **In person** badge on the Clients list, on Train, and when you add them to a session.

## What changes

- No invite link or invite email. Setup's **Invite clients** step leaves them out.
- You log their sessions. See [Running a session for a client](/help/in-the-gym/running-a-session).
- Everything else works as usual: programs, progression, history, compliance and private notes.
- In Messages, Spotter reminds you they don't use the app, so they won't see messages.

## Give them the app later

On their client page, choose **Give app access**. Their invite link appears; send it to them. Their history comes with them when they join.
