Clients join Spotter through an invite from you. There's no sign-up page for clients.

## Send their invite link

Until a client joins, their client page shows **Waiting for (name) to join** with their invite link. Copy it and send it however you usually talk to them: text, email or a DM.

## Invite by email during setup

The last step of setup, **Invite clients**, emails invites to the clients you tick. Each invite goes out in the client's language. Clients you've already invited show when you invited them.

## What your client does

They open the link, pick **English** or **Français**, set a password and start training. If you've already built their program, it's waiting for them. See [Joining your coach on Spotter](/help/for-clients/joining-your-coach).

Once they've joined, the **Invited** badge disappears from the Clients list.

> **Tip** Clients who won't use the app don't need an invite. Mark them **In person only** and log their sessions yourself. See [In-person clients](/help/clients/in-person-clients).

## Messages before they join

You can message a client who hasn't joined yet. They'll see your messages when they do.
