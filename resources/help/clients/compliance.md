## Compliance

A client's compliance is the share of workouts scheduled in the last 30 days that they completed. It shows on the Clients list and on their client page:

- **Green**: 80% or more.
- **Amber**: 50 to 79%.
- **Red**: under 50%.

A client with nothing scheduled in the last 30 days shows a dash instead. Sessions you log for a client in the gym count the same as sessions they log themselves.

## Needs attention

The Overview page lists clients who missed a workout in the last 7 days, or who have nothing scheduled from today on.

## Missed sessions in the inbox

Each morning Spotter also checks for clients who missed two or more sessions in the past week and adds them to their coach's inbox. See [Missed sessions](/help/inbox-messages/missed-sessions).
