Archive a client when you stop coaching them: a break, a finished block, someone who went quiet. Everything they ever logged is kept, so if they come back you pick up exactly where you left off.

## What archiving does

- They leave your **Clients** list, your **Inbox**, **Train** and missed-session alerts.
- Every workout, check-in, habit, message and note is kept.
- They stop counting toward your bill **from the next month**. A client counts for any month they were active in, so archiving on the 20th takes them off next month's bill, not this one.

## Archive a client

1. Open the client's page.
2. Choose **Archive**.
3. Pick what they can do while archived:
    - **Can view their history**: they can still sign in and look back at their workouts, check-ins and messages, but can't log anything new or message you.
    - **Can't sign in**: they're signed out and can't get back in until you unarchive them.
4. Choose **Archive** to confirm.

You can change what they can do any time from the grey box at the top of their page.

## Find and unarchive a client

1. Open **Clients** and choose **Archived**.
2. Open the client and choose **Unarchive**.

They're back in your lists straight away, with everything as it was. Inbox items that were open when you archived them come back too.

## Archive inactive clients automatically

1. Open **Clients** and choose **Archiving**.
2. Under **Archive my clients automatically**, pick 30, 60, 90 or 180 days.
3. Choose what archived clients can do by default, and **Save**.

Each morning, Spotter archives your clients who haven't signed in or completed a session (including one a trainer logged for them in person) for that long. Clients you added recently aren't archived just because they haven't joined yet. Their page shows they were archived for inactivity.

> **Note** Auto-archiving applies to your own clients. In a gym, each coach sets their own.

## Archive or delete?

Archive is for stopping coaching. **Delete** erases the client and all of their history for good, and is only for when someone asks you to erase their data. See [Reassigning or deleting a client](/help/clients/reassigning-and-removing).
