Habits are the things that happen outside the gym: a step target, a 30-minute walk three times a week, two litres of water, protein at breakfast. Clients tick them off on their home screen.

## Add a habit for a client

1. Open the client's page.
2. Under **Habits**, choose **Add**.
3. Name it, then pick how it's tracked:
    - **Tick it off**: done or not, like a walk or stretching.
    - **Log a number**: a value against a daily target, like 8,000 steps or 2 litres. It counts as done once they reach the target.
4. Pick **How often**: every day, or a number of days a week.
5. Choose **Add habit**.

## See how it's going

Each habit shows this week's count against its goal (for example **3 of 5 this week**) and a dot for each of the last 7 days: filled when done, amber when they logged a number under target, empty when nothing was logged. Hover a dot for the day and value.

## Habits clients add themselves

Clients can add their own habits too. They're marked **Their own** on the client's page. Clients can stop their own habits but not the ones you set.

## Stop a habit

Choose the **×** next to it. It disappears from the client's list, and everything they logged is kept.

> **Tip** Start with one or two. A client can have up to 12 at once, but a short list gets done.
