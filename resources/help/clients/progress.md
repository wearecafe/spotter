Open a client's page and choose **Progress** to see every exercise they've logged, across every program and one-off workout.

## The list

Each exercise shows:

- how many sessions they've logged it,
- the change since their first session, as a percentage,
- a small trend line, and
- their **first**, **best** and **latest** numbers.

Exercises they did most recently come first. Choose one to see a chart and every session they've logged it, newest first.

## Choose how progress is measured

At the top, pick one:

| Measure | What it shows | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Heaviest set** | The most weight lifted in a session. | Strength blocks, simple to explain. |
| **Estimated 1-rep max** | What they could likely lift once, worked out from weight and reps. | Comparing sessions with different rep ranges. |
| **Total volume** | Weight × reps, added up across the session. | Hypertrophy work and higher-rep blocks. |

Your choice is saved, and your clients see their own progress the same way. Estimated 1-rep max leaves out sets above 12 reps, where the estimate stops being useful.

## What counts

- Only **completed** sessions count.
- A set ticked done with the load or reps left blank counts as done as prescribed.
- Numbers show in the unit the client logs in now, kilograms or pounds.
- Exercises with no weight logged (bodyweight work) don't appear yet.
