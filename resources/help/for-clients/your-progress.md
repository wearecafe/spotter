Choose **Progress** in the menu to see every exercise you've logged.

Each one shows your **first**, **best** and **latest** numbers, and how much you've improved since your first session. Tap an exercise for a chart and every session you've done it.

Your coach chooses how progress is measured: your heaviest set, your estimated one-rep max, or your total volume (weight × reps). The page says which.

Only workouts you've completed count, so remember to tap **Complete workout** at the end.

> **Tip** Log your weights even when you do exactly what was prescribed: leaving the box blank counts as the prescribed weight, so your progress still shows.
