Open **Nutrition** from the menu. If your coach set daily targets, they're at the top.

## Log a day

1. Pick the date.
2. Enter your **body weight**, **calories**, and **protein**, **carbs** and **fat** in grams. You need at least your weight or your calories.
3. Tap **Save**.

There's one entry per day; saving a day again updates it. Your last 14 days are listed below.

## Import from your tracking app

Under **Import from your tracking app**, pick your app and upload its export:

- **MacroFactor**: the CSV from its data export.
- **MyFitnessPal**: the .zip from its data export, or the Nutrition Summary CSV inside it. MyFitnessPal may require Premium to export.
- **Lose It!**: the food log CSV from its data export.
- **Other**: any CSV with a Date column plus Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat or Weight.

Days already in Spotter are updated, not duplicated, so you can import as often as you like.
