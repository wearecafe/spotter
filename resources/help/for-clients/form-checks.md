---
title: Sending a form check video
summary: Film a set and send it to your coach for feedback.
order: 3
related: for-clients/logging-a-workout, for-clients/messaging-your-coach
---

1. In your workout, tap **Form check** on the exercise.
2. Film a set, or pick a video you've already recorded. Film from the side, about 3 metres away, so your whole body is in view.
3. Add a note if there's something specific to look at ("Knee caves on the last rep?").
4. Tap **Send to coach**.

The button changes to **Sent ✓**. Your coach replies with notes in your messages.

Videos can be up to 100 MB. Only you and your coach can see them.
