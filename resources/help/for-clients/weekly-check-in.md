Your home screen shows **Weekly check-in** with **Due** until you send it for the week. Tap it to start.

## What you fill in

- **Sessions**: how many you did this week (filled in for you).
- **Average sleep** (hours) and **average steps**. Enter these from your watch or phone; wearable sync is coming.
- **Body weight**, in kg or lb.
- **Energy** and **stress**, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
- **Anything else?** Wins, struggles, travel, anything sore.
- **Progress photos**: up to 3, optional. Only you and your coach can see them.

Tap **Send check-in**. Your coach usually replies within a day.

## Change it

Sent something by mistake? Open the check-in again during the same week, change it and tap **Update check-in**.
