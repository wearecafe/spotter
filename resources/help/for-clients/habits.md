Your habits are on your home screen under **Habits**, below today's workout.

## Log a habit

- **Tick-off habits**: tap the circle when you've done it. Tap again to undo.
- **Number habits** (like steps or water): type the number and tap the check. It turns green once you hit your target.

Forgot yesterday? Choose **Yesterday** at the top and log it there.

Each habit shows how this week is going, for example **3 of 5 this week**.

## Add your own habit

1. Under **Habits**, choose **Add**.
2. Name it, pick **Tick it off** or **Log a number** (with a daily target), and how often.
3. Choose **Add habit**.

Your coach can see your habits, including the ones you add. You can stop your own habits with the **×**; the ones your coach set stay until they change them.
