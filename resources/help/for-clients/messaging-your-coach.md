Open **Messages** from the menu, or **Chat with (your coach)** on your home screen.

- Type a message and send. On a computer, **Enter** sends.
- Add up to 4 photos at a time. Only you and your coach can see them.
- **Seen** shows once your coach has read your message.

New messages appear on their own within a few seconds. The menu shows a number when you have unread messages.

## Email when you're away

If a message from your coach is still unread after 10 minutes, Spotter emails you once to let you know, in your language.
