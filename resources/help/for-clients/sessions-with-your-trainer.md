When you train with your coach in the gym, they can log your sets for you as you go. You don't need to do anything.

Afterwards, the session shows in your app:

- **Logged by (your coach) in your session**, with any notes they wrote.
- On your home screen, the session is marked **With your coach**.

It counts just like a workout you logged yourself: it's in your history, and your next sessions are planned from what you lifted.

Want to add how it felt? Open the session and add a note under **How did it go?**
