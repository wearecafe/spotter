1. Open the session.
2. Choose **Move to another day**.
3. Pick a day and choose **Move**.

You can move a session to any day from today to the end of the week it was planned for, so the week's training still happens that week. Your coach is told that you moved it.

## When you can't move one

- **You've started it**: once you've logged a set, it stays on its day.
- **It's from an earlier week**: sessions only move within their own week.
- **No button?** Your coach may have turned moving off for you. Message them and they can move it.
