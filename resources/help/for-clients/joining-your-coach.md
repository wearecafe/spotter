Your coach sends you an invite link by email, text or message.

1. Open the link.
2. Choose your language: **English** or **Français**.
3. Set a password and choose **Start training**.

If your coach has already built your program, it's waiting for you: same exercises, same loads, nothing to redo. If not, your workouts appear as your coach adds them.

## Moving from another app

If your coach is moving from another app, your invite says so. Your past workout logs might not come across, but your coach has your current program and loads.

## Keep it on your phone

Spotter runs in your phone's browser; there's nothing to download. To open it like an app, add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

## Signing in later

Go to the Spotter home page and choose **Log in**, with your email and the password you set. Forgot it? See [Signing in and passwords](/help/account/signing-in).
