## Shared by everyone on the team

- **Custom exercises.** Any coach can use an exercise a teammate added.
- **Workout templates.** Every coach sees the team's templates, marked with who made them. Use **Copy to my templates** to make your own version. Owners and admins can edit any template.
- **The brand** clients see: the team owner's name and colour.

## Each coach's own

- **Clients.** Coaches see their own clients. Owners and admins see everyone's.
- **Inbox.** Each coach's inbox has their own clients' items.
- **Messages.** Coaches message their own clients. Owners and admins can see every conversation.
