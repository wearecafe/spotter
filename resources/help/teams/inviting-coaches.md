Owners and admins invite coaches from **Team**.

1. Under **Invite a coach**, enter their email.
2. Pick their role: **Coach** sees their own clients; **Admin** sees every client and can manage the team.
3. Choose **Send invite**.

They get an email with a link to join. The invitation expires after 14 days.

## Pending invitations

Pending invitations are listed with when they expire and the invitation link, if you'd rather send it yourself. Choose **Revoke** to cancel one. Inviting the same email again sends a fresh link.

## Who can't be invited

- Someone already on your team.
- An email that belongs to a client account.

## When they accept

New coaches create their account straight into your team. A coach who already uses Spotter on their own brings their clients, programs and templates with them. See [Joining a gym's team](/help/teams/joining-a-team).
