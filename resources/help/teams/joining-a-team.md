Open the link in your invitation email.

- **New to Spotter:** enter your name and a password and choose **Create account and join**.
- **Already on Spotter:** log in with the email the invitation was sent to, then accept.

## What comes with you

If you've been coaching on your own in Spotter, your clients, programs and templates come with you into the team.

## When you can't join

- **The invitation was sent to another email.** Log out and sign in with that account.
- **You own a team with other coaches.** Remove them first, or ask Spotter support to merge teams.
- **You're a coach on another team.** Ask an admin there to remove you first. Your clients stay with that team.
- **It's a client account.** Client accounts can't join a coaching team.
