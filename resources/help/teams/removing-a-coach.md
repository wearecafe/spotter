---
title: Removing a coach
summary: Take a coach off your team and hand their clients to someone else.
order: 3
related: teams/roles, clients/reassigning-and-removing
---

1. Open **Team** and choose **Remove** next to the coach.
2. Under **Hand their clients to**, pick who takes over.
3. Choose **Remove coach**.

Their clients move to the coach you picked, with their programs, upcoming workouts and history. The removed coach keeps their Spotter account, now as a solo workspace of their own, without your clients.

The team owner can't be removed.

> **Tip** To move just a few clients between coaches, reassign them one at a time from each client's page. See [Reassigning or removing a client](/help/clients/reassigning-and-removing).
