When you train someone in person, run their session in Spotter and log it for them. It counts exactly like a session they logged themselves.

## Start the session

Either:

- Open **Train**, tick the client and choose **Start session**, or
- Open the client's page and choose **Run session**.

Spotter opens today's session. If they have nothing today, their next session moves to today. See [When nothing is scheduled today](/help/in-the-gym/nothing-scheduled-today).

## Log as you go

Each exercise shows the prescription, any notes, and **Last time**: what the client did the last time they did that exercise, like "3 × 5 @ 75 kg".

For each set, enter **reps**, **load** and **RPE**, and tick **Done**. Blank boxes show the prescription.

Every change saves as you enter it, so nothing is lost if your phone locks between sets. Close the page and come back from Train or the client page to carry on.

## Rest timer

Ticking a set done starts a rest timer with the exercise's prescribed rest. It shows at the bottom of the screen with **+15s** and **Skip**. Phones that support it vibrate when rest is up.

## Session notes

Write cues and observations in **Session notes**. The client sees them with the session in their app.

## Finish

Choose **Finish (name)'s session**. If you need to change something afterwards, choose **Reopen**.

> **Tip** Need to add an exercise or change the plan mid-session? Choose **Edit workout**, make the change, then go back. Your logged sets are kept.
