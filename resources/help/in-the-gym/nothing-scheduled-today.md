---
title: When nothing is scheduled today
summary: How Spotter moves a client's next session to today when you start training them.
order: 3
related: in-the-gym/running-a-session, programming/editing-one-session
---

On **Train**, clients without a session today show their next one, like "Next: Upper A, Thu (moves to today)".

When you start the session, that next session moves to today. For a program session:

- It keeps its exercises and planned loads.
- It's marked **Edited**, so program changes won't move it back to its old day.
- The rest of the program stays on its usual days.

If a client has nothing scheduled at all, Spotter says so. Build them a workout or program first.
