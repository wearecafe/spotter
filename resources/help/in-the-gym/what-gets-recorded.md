## For the client

The session shows **Logged by (your name) in your session**, with your session notes. On their home screen, past sessions you logged are marked **With your coach**.

## On their client page

Their history shows **Logged by (your name)** and your session notes.

## Progression and compliance

A session you log counts exactly like one the client logs: it re-plans their program's coming weeks and counts towards their compliance.

## Your inbox

You were there, so a session you log doesn't add inbox items for you (no feedback or new-best items). If a teammate covers the session for you, new personal bests still reach your inbox as the client's coach.
