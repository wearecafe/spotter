## Start a group session

1. Open **Train**.
2. Tick everyone in the session. People with a session today are listed first; search **Everyone else** for the rest.
3. Choose **Start group session**.

## Switch between clients

Each client has a tab at the top with how many sets they've done (like 6/14). Tap a tab to switch. Each client's own session, prescriptions and **Last time** numbers are on their tab.

Each client has their own rest timer. When several are running, all of them show at the bottom of the screen with the client's name; tap one to jump to that client. A dot on a client's tab shows their rest is running.

## Add or drop someone

- **Add client** brings someone else into the session. Their session for today joins, or their next session moves to today.
- The **×** on a client's session takes them out of this screen. Nothing they've logged is lost.

## Finish

Finish each client when they're done, or choose **Finish everyone** at the end.
