title: Clearing the inbox on your phone
summary: Go through items one at a time with big buttons, between clients.
order: 3
related: inbox-messages/coach-inbox
---

On the Inbox, choose **One at a time**. Each item fills the screen with its summary, the draft reply, and the actions:

- **Approve and send**
- **Done**
- **Snooze** (until tomorrow morning)
- **Skip** to come back to it later

A counter shows how many you've done. When you reach the end, the inbox is clear.
