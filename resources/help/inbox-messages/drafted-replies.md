## Drafts

When an item arrives, Spotter reads what the client sent along with their recent training, then writes:

- a short **summary** for you, and
- a **reply** in the client's language.

Drafts are written by AI when it's turned on for Spotter (it uses your recent messages as a guide to your tone), and by built-in rules otherwise. Either way it's only a draft: edit it freely, or choose **Redraft** for a fresh one. Nothing is sent until you choose **Approve and send**.

## Suggested changes

Sometimes the draft suggests a change to the client's next workout: one fewer set after a rough week, for example. It shows what changes, from what to what, and why. Choose **Apply** to make the change, or ignore it.

Applying changes only that next session, not the program.
