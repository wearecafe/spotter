---
title: Missed sessions
summary: When a client is flagged for missing sessions, and what counts as missed.
order: 4
related: clients/compliance, inbox-messages/coach-inbox
---

A session is **missed** when its date has passed and it wasn't completed.

Every morning at 6 a.m., Spotter checks each client. If they missed **two or more** sessions in the past 7 days, a **Missed sessions** item goes into their coach's inbox, with a friendly check-in message drafted. A client is flagged at most once a week.

Sessions you skipped on purpose don't count as missed. See [Changing or skipping a single session](/help/programming/editing-one-session).
