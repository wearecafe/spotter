Open **Messages** to see every conversation, newest first, with unread counts. Search by client name. Owners and admins see the whole gym's conversations.

## Sending

- Type a message and send. On a computer, **Enter** sends.
- Add up to 4 photos at a time. Photos are private to you and the client.
- **Seen** shows under your last message once the client has read it.

New messages appear on their own within a few seconds.

## Email reminders

If a message is still unread after 10 minutes, Spotter emails the recipient once to let them know. After that, it waits at least an hour before emailing them again. Clients who haven't joined yet aren't emailed.

## Messages and the inbox

A client's message also shows in your Inbox with a drafted reply. Replying from either place clears both.

## Clients who don't use the app

For in-person clients and clients who haven't joined, Messages tells you so. See [In-person clients](/help/clients/in-person-clients).
