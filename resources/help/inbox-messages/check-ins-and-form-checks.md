## Weekly check-ins

Once a week, clients send a check-in from their app: body weight, average sleep and steps, energy and stress (1 to 5), notes, and up to 3 progress photos. It takes about two minutes.

Each check-in lands in your inbox. The summary compares it with their last check-in and includes how many sessions they did and their nutrition for the week.

## Form checks

While logging a workout, clients can film a set and send it with a note ("Knee caves on the last rep?"). It lands in your inbox as a **Form check** with the video.

Photos and videos are private: only the client and the coaches who manage them can see them.
