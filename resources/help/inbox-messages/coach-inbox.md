The **Inbox** gathers what your clients do that's worth a reply. Each item comes with a short summary and a drafted reply you can edit.

## What lands in the inbox

| Item | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Check-in** | A client sends their weekly check-in. |
| **Form check** | A client sends a video of a set. |
| **Missed sessions** | A client missed two or more sessions in the past week. |
| **Workout feedback** | A client finishes a workout and leaves a note. |
| **Message** | A client messages you. New messages refresh the same item instead of piling up. |
| **New best** | A client lifts more than ever before on an exercise. |

Items are sorted so the most urgent come first. Filter by type at the top.

## Deal with an item

- **Approve and send**: sends your reply (edited or not) to the client as a message, and clears the item.
- **Done, no reply**: clears it without sending anything.
- **Snooze**: hides it until tomorrow at 8 a.m.
- **Apply**: makes a suggested change to their next workout. See [Drafted replies and suggested changes](/help/inbox-messages/drafted-replies).

Nothing reaches the client until you approve it.

## Clients you can see

You see items for your own clients. Each coach in a gym has their own inbox.
